Key Selling Points

What to Lead With in Every Conversation

These are your strongest angles for restaurant owners. Speed of service, fewer kitchen mistakes, and never missing a phone order are the hooks.

🤖

AI Answers the Phone

Say This

"How many phone orders do you miss on a Friday night because everyone's slammed? Aldelo's Voice Order AI picks up every call, 24/7, takes the order, and sends it straight to the POS. Your staff stays focused on the guests in front of them."

  • Voice Order AI answers calls 24/7, even multiple at once
  • Orders flow directly into the POS and kitchen
  • AI voice reservations too
  • Frees up staff during rush
  • Data on what callers ask for and order
📱

Tableside Ordering & Payment

Say This

"Your servers are walking back and forth to a terminal all night. With the handheld, they take the order at the table, it hits the kitchen instantly, and the guest pays right there. Faster table turns, and the card never leaves the customer's sight."

  • ePay Terminal P5L — POS and payments in one handheld
  • Tap, tip, swipe, and mobile wallet
  • Orders fire to kitchen from the table
  • Pay at the table — faster turns, fewer chargebacks
  • Works for curbside, drive-thru, and patio
👨‍🍳

Kitchen Display & Hold and Fire

Say This

"Lost tickets and wrong orders cost you food and comps. The kitchen display routes every item to the right station, tracks prep time, and color-codes what's late. And hold and fire means apps and entrées come out when they should."

  • KDS modes: Line Cook, Expediter, Traditional, Consolidated
  • Auto-routes items to the right station
  • Prep timers and color-coded order status
  • Hold and fire + meal coursing for full service
  • Up to 20 KDS screens and 20 kitchen printers
⚡

Fast Payments That Work Offline

Say This

"What happens when your internet drops on a Saturday night? With most systems, you stop taking cards. Aldelo has an offline mode that keeps taking payments and syncs when the connection comes back."

  • Transactions in about 3 seconds
  • Offline Mode stores encrypted transactions until back online
  • PCI Level 1 and PCI SSF certified
  • Built for high-volume peak hours
  • POS-integrated or stand-alone
💳

Cash Discount & Tip Automation

Say This

"Restaurants run on thin margins, and card fees eat into every check. The cash discount program can take a big bite out of those fees. And Tip Adjust AI handles tip adjustments so closing out doesn't take all night."

  • Built-in cash discount program
  • Tip Adjust AI speeds up end-of-night closeout
  • AI-assisted ticket closeouts
  • Split and combine checks
  • Customizable receipts, email and SMS receipts
🎁

Loyalty, Gift Cards & Online Ordering

Say This

"Every guest who comes back is cheaper than finding a new one. Aldelo has loyalty, gift cards, and online ordering built in, so you can run it all from one system instead of paying for three separate apps."

  • Points, tiers, and rewards loyalty programs
  • Physical and virtual gift cards, multi-store
  • Branded online ordering
  • Delivery module with caller ID integration
  • Self-order kiosk and customer display available

Cloud Reporting & Labor Management

Say This

"You can't be in the restaurant every hour. Aldelo is cloud-based, so you can check sales, labor, and inventory from your phone. Scheduling and time cards are built in, and Face ID login stops buddy punching."

  • Real-time sync, remote access, automatic backups

  • Inventory countdown — 86 items automatically
  • Menu Builder AI for fast menu setup

Objection Handling

When They Push Back, Say This

The most common objections from restaurant owners.

"We already have Toast (or Square, or Clover)."
"That's fine — a lot of our restaurants came from those. Let me ask: is your system answering your phone for you? Does it keep taking cards when the internet goes down? Aldelo does both, and there's 24/7 live support when something breaks on a Friday night."
"What happens if the internet goes out?"
"Aldelo has an Offline Mode. It keeps taking card payments, stores them encrypted on the device, and processes them once you're back online. You don't stop selling."
"I don't trust AI taking my phone orders."
"Totally fair. Think of it this way — right now, when you're slammed, some calls just don't get answered, and that's lost money. The AI picks up every call, even several at once, and puts the order right into the POS. You can start with it only during rush hours and see how it does."
"My staff won't want to learn a new system."
"It runs on the iPad or Android devices they already know how to use. Big picture menus, Face ID login, and the handheld works like a phone. Most staff are comfortable after a shift or two, and support is there 24/7 if they get stuck."
"How much does it cost?"
"It depends on how many stations, handhelds, and kitchen screens you need. Let's walk through your setup — counter, floor, kitchen — and I'll build the quote. And with the cash discount program, a lot of restaurants offset a big chunk of their card fees, which helps pay for the system."
"We're under contract with our current processor."
"Let's look at when it's up and what the early termination fee is. Often the savings from the cash discount program cover that fee in a few months. If not, we can get everything lined up so you're ready to switch the day it ends."
Qualifying the Prospect

Questions to Ask Early in the Call

These help you figure out which bundle fits and how many devices they need.

1

Service Style

"Is it counter service, table service, or both?" — Counter leans Android or iPad station. Table service means handhelds for every server.

2

Stations & Servers

"How many registers do you run, and how many servers are on the floor on a busy night?" — This sets the device count. One ePay Terminal P5L per server is an easy add-on.

3

Kitchen Setup

"How do orders get to the kitchen right now — printed tickets or screens?" — If paper, pitch the KDS. Ask how many stations they have on the line.

4

Phone & Delivery

"How many phone orders do you take, and do you deliver?" — Heavy phone volume is your Voice Order AI pitch. Pizza and delivery shops get caller ID and the delivery module.

5

Monthly Card Volume

"Roughly how much do you run in card sales a month?" — Sets up the cash discount conversation and the savings number.

6

Current System & Contract

"What are you using now, and when is your contract up?" — Find the pain with their current setup and plan around any termination fee.
